## Part 1: the real coaches (what they teach, how they talk)

### 1. Eddy Reynoso (Canelo): the counterpunching Mexican
- Teaches counterpunching, which is rare in Mexican boxing, built on old-school fundamentals. Grew up admiring counterpunchers `[src]`.
- **Builds a counter for one man's habit and drills it to death.** Vs Saunders: "he leans forward when he wants to throw the straight left, so step back and counter with the uppercut", "repetition after repetition until you perfect it" `[src]`.
- Body hook is identity: "if you don't know how to land a good hook to the body, you're not a Mexican fighter" `[src]`. Head hook then straight to the body, or an uppercut down the middle `[src]`.
- Learned from losses and adapted `[src]`.
- Sources: [TSS Trainer of the Year](https://tss.ib.tv/boxing/eddy-reynoso-is-the-2021-tss-trainer-of-the-year), [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/32591), [Evolve breakdown](https://evolve-mma.com/blog/breaking-down-canelo-alvarezs-style-of-boxing/)

### 2. Brian "Bomac" McIntyre (Crawford): the lifer who reads the man
- Lifelong pairing: coached the same fighter from his teens through his whole pro career `[src]`.
- Studies the opponent for hours. "Speed and timing beat power". Doesn't put much value in sparring partners `[src]`.
- Calm and confident, almost dismissive. Called the Canelo win "easy work" `[src]`. Crawford's identity is the read (downloads a man in 2–3 rounds) and the switch `[mem]` (see `mind_p4p_a.md`).
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_McIntyre), [WBC story](https://wbcboxing.com/en/from-boxer-to-mentor-the-story-of-bomc-and-terence-crawford), [Ring: "easy work"](https://ringmagazine.com/en/news/terence-crawford-s-trainer-describes-canelo-victory-as-easy-work)

### 3. SugarHill Steward (Kronk): get him before he gets you
- Kronk school (nephew of Emanuel Steward): **go for the knockout**, a thudding jab and a heavy right hand. Believes power can be taught (sitting down on punches) `[src]`.
- Mostly basics in camp, encourages aggressive sparring `[src]`.
- Plans in short punch scripts: "Jab, and then a feint and a jab, boom!" `[src]`.
- Sources: [Boxing News: Kronk vs Wilder](https://so.affino.com/interviews/how-tyson-fury-battered-deontay-wilder-with-the-kronk-style-reveals-sugarhill-steward), [Sky: the Kronk way](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12040/13137248/fury-vs-usyk-a-first-round-knockdown-with-a-jab-tyson-fury-to-follow-the-kronk-way-against-oleksandr-usyk)

### 4. Derrick James (Spence, Charlo): the teacher who doesn't yell
- "A trainer gets you in shape, a coach yells, a teacher teaches." **Good teaching means he rarely has to yell** `[src]`. A stickler for fundamentals.
- Very heavy sparring: 14–18 rounds a session, sometimes 20 `[src]`. Mimics the next opponent on the mitts `[src]`.
- Turned a classic boxer (Charlo) aggressive, accurate and powerful, with real adjustments between rematches `[src]`.
- Sources: [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/31239), [3Kings Trainer of the Year](https://3kingsboxing.com/derrick-james-trainer-year/)

### 5. Freddie Roach (Pacquiao): mitts, numbers, angles
- Record five BWAA Trainer of the Year awards `[src]`.
- Mitts are everything: one drill repeated (jab-uppercut-hook, three rounds, three times a day) to build the weak side `[src]`.
- **Calls combos by number**, and the fighter throws them without thinking `[src]`. Plays the opponent on the mitts, slipping and sliding to teach cutting off the ring `[src]`.
- Feint instead of a punch to set things up. Fast flurries for hand speed `[src]`. Game plans get built in conversation on the pads `[src]`.
- Sources: [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/32254), [Philstar: working mitts](https://philstar.com/sports/2014/11/23/1394908/working-mitts-roach)

### 6. Robert Garcia (Oxnard): Mexican pressure, fix it in the corner
- Three-time BWAA Trainer of the Year (2024 with Bam Rodriguez and Vergil Ortiz) `[src]`.
- Attack-minded, close-range Mexican pressure `[src]`. Pushes fighters to go for the KO (Joshua camp) `[src]`.
- **A real mid-fight fix:** Ortiz threw 30 jabs in rounds 8–9, Garcia told him to jab, and he threw 99 in rounds 10–12, "to get in and out with combinations" `[src]`.
- Builds a gym culture `[src]`.
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Garcia_(American_boxer)), [BoxingScene: winning culture](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/training-grounds-robert-garcia-created-winning-culture), [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/pro-boxing/news-anthony-joshua-will-ko-usyk-says-robert-garcia)

### 7. Shingo Inoue (Naoya Inoue): "he's not a genius, he's drilled"
- **The Ring 2025 Trainer of the Year** `[src]`.
- "People who call Naoya a genius are only seeing the results of training." The same advice for years, weak spots drilled until fixed `[src]`.
- Fundamentals: basic combos reviewed over and over. **Pull the hand back to guard right after the punch. Finish the combo reset and guarded against the counter** `[src]`. Father and son.
- Sources: [Nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-topics/g00734/the-monster-shaking-up-the-world-of-boxing.html), [Ring 2025 awards](https://www.ringmagazine.com/news/2025-ring-awards-shingo-inoue-is-trainer-of-year-4hwBTnSComQfSe3bAQMQEs)

### 8. Anatoly Lomachenko (Loma, Usyk): the scientist of movement
- Father-coach. Folk-dance (hopak) classes for footwork, ladder drills `[src]`.
- **Brain training:** math problems when exhausted, chess, juggling, handstands, breath-holds. "Movement intelligence" `[src]`.
- Loma's identity: angles, pivots, and a read that turns a fight in the middle rounds `[mem]` (`mind_p4p_a.md`).
- Sources: [SI: dance classes](https://www.si.com/boxing/2018/05/09/vasiliy-lomachenko-boxing-footwork-dancing-jorge-linares), [Boxing Science](https://boxingscience.co.uk/lomachenko-training-methods/)

### 9. Wali Moses (Shakur Stevenson): low risk, always a plan B
- Defense-first, low-risk boxing `[src]`. **"Go in with a backup plan if the main one fails"** `[src]`.
- Raises the risk only when it's safe: vs Lopez, "once he couldn't keep up with our hand speed, we took more risks" `[src]`. Critics call the style boring `[src]`.
- Source: [Ring: Lopez's biggest mistake](https://www.ringmagazine.com/news/shakur-stevenson-s-trainer-explains-teofimo-lopez-s-biggest-mistake-25PKH8nDKAA4xBsreHs0Pa)

### 10. Virgil Hunter (Andre Ward): inside, dirty, mental
- Inside clinch work and outside work **drilled every day**, and that became Ward's style `[src]`.
- Trains to discomfort and pain so the fighter is ready for the heat `[src]`. Rarely raises his voice `[src]`.
- Between rounds vs Kovalev: "don't let him breathe, don't let him get off" `[src]`. Studies the opponent and builds a game plan.
- Sources: [BoxingScene: Ward](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/andre-ward-overlook-abraham-wants-bute-shot), [WBA: Ward vs Kovalev](https://www.wbaboxing.com/boxing-news/ward-ekes-out-decision-over-kovalev)

### 11. Shane McGuigan (UK): the sports scientist
- Two-time BBBofC / Boxing News Trainer of the Year `[src]`. Studied under Poliquin and covers boxing, S&C and nutrition himself `[src]`.
- Boxing first every day, and the S&C serves the boxing. Interval work over long runs. Leg strength because fighters move more now `[src]`.
- Pads are where the realest movement gets learned `[src]`. Pushes "let your hands go in combinations, don't set on the jab" `[src]`.
- Sources: [Boxing News: Shane vs Barry](https://boxingnewsonline.net/features/changing-times-shane-and-barry-mcguigan-compare-how-they-train/), [Shane on Haye](https://boxingnewsonline.net/features/shane-mcguigan-on-training-david-haye/), [Trainer of the year](https://boxingshowtimes.com/boxing-news/shane-mcguigan-trainer-of-the-year)

### 12. Don Charles (Dubois): walk him down
- Boxing News Trainer of the Year 2025 (Dubois beat Hrgovic and Joshua) `[src]`.
- Corner words: **"creep forward, with pressure, make him breathe heavy", "walk him down; use your right hand to parry; shield with your left"** `[src]`.
- Jab plus power as the identity. Admits it when the plan fails (Usyk took the jab battle) `[src]`.
- Sources: [Boxing News award](https://boxingnewsonline.net/don-charles-wins-boxing-news-trainer-of-the-year-award/), [BoxingScene: Project Dynamite](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/project-dynamite-builders-behind-renovation-daniel-dubois)

### 13. Buddy McGirt (Gatti, Whyte): don't change him, polish him
- **Learns your style and adds small things. Doesn't change it.** Starts by asking a fighter which punches and fighters he likes `[src]`.
- Defense and counters, inside defense. "Get your punches off and get out of there", don't sit and wait for the return fire `[src]`.
- "Very nice but a very strict disciplinarian" `[src]`. Light on film: said he never watched Itauma's tape `[src]`.
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_McGirt), [Ring: Itauma tape claim](https://ringmagazine.com/en/news/moses-itauma-fires-back-buddy-mcgirt-tape-study-claim), [BoxingScene: Penalosa](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/penalosa-praises-mcgirt)

### 14. Teddy Atlas (Cus D'Amato school): the fire speech
- More commentator than trainer now, but he's the archetype of the emotional corner `[src]`.
- Fear is the subject: "everyone's nature is to avoid danger". The trainer leads the fighter "into a dark place" `[src]`. "We are firemen! The heat doesn't bother us" (Bradley 2015) `[src]`.
- Tough love and parables. Criticized as over-dramatic. "I did what I believed the fighter needed" `[src]`.
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddy_Atlas), [BOXRAW: 36](https://boxraw.com/blogs/blog/preview-36-by-teddy-atlas)

**Thin or missing (search later):** Ben Davison (only his CV turned up: Fury, Taylor, Haney, Wood), Usyk's head coach (Yegor Golub; results only covered S&C coach Jakub Chycki and Schulte-table eye drills), Kay Koroma, Joel Diaz ("smother, work, throw back with volume"), Manny Robles, Joe Gallagher, Bivol's team (Gennadiy Mashyanov).

## Part 2: what makes trainers different (the axes)

Pulled from the cards above. Every game trainer is a point on these:

| Axis | Low end | High end |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan lean** (which `STRATS` he picks) | counter / move (Reynoso, Moses) | pressure / ko (Garcia, Steward, Charles) |
| **Teaching speed** (`TEACH` multiplier) | polish only (McGirt) | drills it in (Inoue, Roach, James) |
| **Scouting** (prep for this man) | barely watches tape (McGirt) | builds a counter for his habit (Reynoso, McIntyre, James) |
| **Corner voice** | calm, rarely yells (James, Hunter, McIntyre) | fire speech (Atlas, Steward) |
| **Adjusting** (mid-fight change) | sticks to the plan | one sharp fix (Garcia's jab, Moses's plan B) |
| **Changes the fighter's style?** | keeps it (McGirt) | remakes it (James turned Charlo aggressive) |
| **Camp focus** | sparring volume (James) / pads (Roach, McGuigan) / S&C (McGuigan) / brain drills (Lomachenko) | |
| **Risk** | low risk, raise it when safe (Moses) | get him before he gets you (Kronk) |

## Part 4: how it could plug in (when the brain is done)

Most of this lands on systems that already exist:
- **`cornerAdvice()`** picks one line from a fixed checklist. Each trainer gets his own **priority order** and his own **line bank** (voice). A pressure coach reads "too passive" first; a defensive coach reads "he's beating you to the punch" first.
- **`aiStrategy()`** (the AI corner when you leave it on auto) gets a per-trainer weight on `STRATS`. That also gives **opponents** a real corner: Hill Sewell's fighter comes out swinging in round 8.
- **`teachK` / `TEACH`**: a per-trainer multiplier on how fast lessons stick and how slowly they fade.
- **`talkPrompt()`**: add the trainer's name and philosophy, so the fighter's reply fits ("Yeah coach, hands home").
- **Camp** (career + gym mode): what the trainer drills shows up in skill-level mastery (L4 learning): Roach → combos, James → sparring XP, Lomov → footwork, Reyes → a counter built for the next opponent.
- **Skill levels (BRAIN L1–L6)** are the natural hook: trainers decide WHICH skills grow fastest. Builds after L4.
- **Gym mode**: the player IS the trainer there. Option: Ed's gym hires assistant coaches from this list, or the player picks a philosophy.
- **Parking-lot items this absorbs:** "fire him up / calm him down / tell him the truth", combo numbers in corner talk, AI corner shouts, opponent-specific camp, bond/trust (trainer–fighter fit).
