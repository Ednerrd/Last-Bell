## Summary (rows with a usable number; rough)

**Fight # at first title shot**
- Everyone (about 24 with a number): median about 16, range 2-37.
- Olympian / elite amateur (about 17): median about 13, range 2-31. Typical cluster: 6-18.
- Little or no pedigree (about 7: Fury, Canelo, Crawford, Charlo, Munguia, Rodriguez, Fulton): median about 23, range 16-37.
- Heavy / cruiser / light heavy (about 6): median about 13 (Usyk 10, Joshua 16, Fury 25, Beterbiev and Bivol about 11).
- Lighter than 175 (about 18): median about 16, range 2-37.

**Years from debut to the shot**
- Everyone: median about 3.4 yrs, range 0.4-6.9.
- Olympian / elite: median about 3.0 yrs (0.4-6.3). Non-pedigree: median about 6.0 yrs (4.5-6.9).

**Age at first shot:** typically 20-27, median about 25. Youngest 20 (Canelo, Inoue, Benavidez). Oldest about 32 (Beterbiev, Katie Taylor, late-starting Olympians).

**Lost the first shot:** 1 of about 24 (Lomachenko, fight 2, a split decision). Nearly all fighters who get a shot are made favorites, so they win it.
**Losses before the first shot:** about 0 of 24. In this sample nobody with a loss on the record got a shot early. Caveat: the sample is champions, so it is survivor-biased (fighters who lost early and never got a shot are not here).

## Takeaways for the game

1. **Fight 15-25 is the right window for a shot.** Elite prospects get one around fight 6-18. Non-pedigree men take until about fight 20-37 and roughly 5-7 years. A player who peaks at fights 15-25 and never gets a shot in 50 is badly off the real curve.
2. **Pedigree is a clock.** Olympians and elite amateurs shave about 3 years / 8-10 fights off the path. The game should give the draft pick or amateur background a faster ladder, not a better body.
3. **Real fighters are almost always unbeaten when they get the shot.** A loss before the shot should push the title back a lot. Promoters protect prospects and fight winners.
4. **Offer the shot as the player's reward for a win streak and a top-10 rank, not a flat fight count.** An early shot (under fight 10) should be rare and sometimes a loss (Lomachenko). A first shot at fights 15-25 should win most of the time for a fighter who got there unbeaten.
5. **Age at first shot is mostly 20-27.** The career should reach a first shot by about 4-5 pro years, with the average fighter at 25-ish and a late-comer window out to 30+.
