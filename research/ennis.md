# Jaron "Boots" Ennis: four fights from the broadcast (Oct 21, Ed's paste)
Source: DAZN commentary + corner audio Ed pasted (Ennis vs Avanesyan, vs Chukhadzhian II, vs Stanionis, vs Lima). Notes in our own words. [S] = said once on the broadcast, [M] = said several times / across fights.

## Who he is
- A switch-hitting boxer-puncher: orthodox and southpaw look the same on him, "the normal eye can't see" the switch [M]. Offense first, a reactive counter-puncher when he wants to be, leads when he wants to [M]. Our closest: the boxer-puncher with the `switch` special.
- Big for the weight (74" reach, 6" more than Stanionis), a real chin: hit clean several times, never hurt badly, comes back with a better shot [M].
- His hole, named in every fight: **the head stays in the same place, up, too long** after he punches; he stays in the pocket and "takes one to give one"; gets lazy dropping the left when he's winning [M]. Commentators: fine vs B-level men, a problem vs the A-list.

## The jab
- **The jab changes levels: chin, chest, belly** (and a power jab, a sniper jab), knees bent on the low ones [S]. A triple jab [S].
- vs Stanionis (a top jabber: ~10 landed a round at ~36%) the longer jab kept Stanionis's jab off: "fended off the jab", kept it at the middle of the range [S].
- The corner, both fights: "you ain't using the jab", "keep the jab popping", "soften him with jabs, run him into something big later" [M]. Commentary: not enough jabs = his offense *and* defense suffer [S].

## Body then head (his signature)
- Touches upstairs to lift the guard and elbows, then the body shot; the body shots then open the uppercut "down the middle" [M]. Avanesyan: lower left side red from body shots by R2, a corner stop in R5. Stanionis: body all fight, the left uppercut drops him R6, corner stop.
- **High guard / shell hunched over: the uppercut** comes up through it, the body around it [M]. Real quote of the idea: shell men (Whitaker... Cotto types) don't get KO'd, they get outpointed and bruised by a man content to hit the gloves, go around them and stab the body [S]. Even blocked, his power moves the man back [S].
- Three uppercuts with the same hand: two head, one body [S].

## How his opponents fought him
- **Punch in between his shots** (Avanesyan, Chukhadzhian): you can't match the speed, so you fire while he's punching. It lands (both clipped him), and it backfired too: the KD on Avanesyan was his counter landing as Avanesyan fired back [M].
- **Chukhadzhian rematch, the 180:** fight 1 he moved 12 rounds, one shot and out, lost 120-108 x3. Fight 2 he came forward, pressured, followed up when he landed, stayed in the pocket, got angles (hit then spin out), the chopping left hook timed on Ennis's bounce-in, held when hurt, took a knee to stop an onslaught (veteran move). Lost a decision but won rounds [M]. Holding: several warnings, then a point taken in R10, and after it "the fight opened up" [S].
- Stanionis (a pressure man behind a high guard, slow feet, cuts the ring going to his right): couldn't track him; only landed when he threw *with* Ennis and both were stationary [S]. Ennis went straight back to the center whenever he neared the ropes [S].
- Lima (southpaw): exchange of lefts, Ennis's uppercut/right hook first; two KDs in R1, the ref stops it [S]. Southpaws hate fighting southpaws: Ennis switched to it [S].

## Corners
- Ennis's dad/trainer: "get him out of there" for 11 rounds; commentary calls it counterproductive: push a man for the KO and he's open to getting dropped himself; give specifics, not a wish [M]. Real calls heard: "catch him jumping in", "catch him in between the shots", "bring your hands back", "stop falling in", "dab fake when he comes in", "move your head, slip, boom boom" [M].
- Stanionis's corner: "if you throw the jab, throw something behind it; let the hands go when you exchange" [S]. Pundit: get physical, take the shots on the way in, rip the body [S].
- Both corners stopped fights between rounds (Avanesyan R5, Stanionis R6): a beaten man, a corner that knows what one beating does to a career [M].

## For Last Bell

2. **Punching in between his shots** = the outclassed man's plan: fire while he's punching. Our counters already do it; as a *plan* for the underdog (the AI corner's call when outclassed) with the cost shown: you trade into his better counter.
3. **The rematch 180** (mover -> pressure, one-and-out -> follow up) is rematch memory L4c territory: the loser's lesson that works ("a plan that holds 3+ rounds", parking lot L4 leftovers). A clean real case.
4. **Shell / high guard vs body + uppercut:** checks our `high` guard (up .75, body .8) and the smart hunter's pick vs a shell (`hurt.md`): matches.
5. **A flaw for the traits pass:** "head stays on the line after he punches" (he admires it, stays in the pocket). Fits the flaw list in ROADMAP item 4; the fix a good corner calls: "bring your hands back / move your head".
6. **The ring center habit:** a good mover goes back to the middle when he nears the ropes, not after he's pinned. Our `gen` (ring general drift) is that; worth a look for the out-boxer's ropes time vs the pressure men (9-10 s/rd).
