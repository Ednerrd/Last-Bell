# Sub-styles inside the 5 base styles (Oct 21, research for the traits pass)

The sub-types below come from a few real sources (Wikipedia style lists, Ring, Skillr, Evolve, a few breakdown clips) plus common fight-fan vocabulary. Where a sub-type is my own grouping from known fighters and no page names it, it is tagged [?]. Tags: [S] one source, [M] several agree, [?] unsure / my own read. ## How the sources cut the styles
- Wikipedia lists 4 offensive styles (swarmer, out-boxer, slugger, boxer-puncher) and puts counterpuncher, southpaw and switch-hitter in "other categories". Counterpuncher is a method, not a range. [S]
- Fight-fan lists often file counters under whichever base fits (Mayweather as out-boxer, Canelo as counter, Lomachenko as out-boxer on Wikipedia and boxer-puncher on the same page). The same man shows up in 2-3 buckets. [M]
- Labels are loose: "pressure fighter", "swarmer", "volume puncher", "crowder", "in-fighter" are used as synonyms. [M]
- Ring warns that country styles (Cuban, Soviet) are generalizations with exceptions (Gamboa, Provodnikov). [S]
- So the sub-types below are what the 3-axis model (range, output, initiative; skillr.md) predicts, checked against fighters people actually name.

## Out-boxer subs
- **Stick-and-move / ring mover (Ali, Leonard, Pep, Plant).** Long range, circles, jab leads, moderate output, starter. Moves to score and to stay off the ropes. Weak to a man who cuts the ring, and to cardio. [M: Evolve, Wikipedia]
- **Ring general / jabber (Holmes, Lewis, Haney).** Controls with a heavy jab, feints, a few short bursts, ties up when he is not the one attacking (skillr.md: Lewis). Out / sniper / starter. Beaten by feints and reach-getters; low risk but can look passive. [S Skillr, plus Wikipedia lists]
- **Long-guard tall jabber (Klitschko, Fury, Bivol, Joshua).** Posts the lead hand, jab and traffic, clinch when closed on. See longguard.md. Weak to feints, body to the lead side, a man who gets under the arm. [M]
- **Slick defensive boxer, shell or roll (Mayweather, Whitaker, Stevenson).** Light output, Philly shell or roll, step-back counters; the shell limits what he can punch, so he uses ring IQ and a flicking jab to steer. [S FightCamp] Whitaker's feet were part of his offense, not just defense (pivot to the side, hook or looping jab over the jab). [S]. Output: Stevenson and Mayweather fight in spurts; judges punish too little (Stevenson vs De Los Santos was one of the lowest-output title fights). [S Boxing Explainers] Weak to relentless body pressure (Zepeda vs Stevenson, fighters_a.md) and to a plan that makes him lose rounds by not throwing.
- **Cuban sniper, low-output counter mover (Rigondeaux, Lara, Scull).** Pawing jab, high guard, jab-cross, wait for the counter, open up only when the other man is hurt or beaten. Ring says the problem is judges: Scull vs Canelo had record-low punches, Rigondeaux lost a split to Casimero throwing little. [M: Evolve, Ring, Boxing Explainers] Weak to the pro scorecards and to a man who keeps coming.
- **Soviet / amateur out-volume boxer (Lomachenko; Whitaker and Haney partly).** Moves, throws a lot, angles off the jab (pivot after the jab), uses feints and vision-blocking jabs. Lomachenko threw about 65 a round at Linares (fighters_a.md) vs the typical 50. Trainer Jackson: like Whitaker, throws a lot, but is the offensive one and punishes a cautious man. [S ESPN, plus repo] Weak to head-lean entries timed by a patient jab and a hook (Lopez), and to the body (Haney landed 50 to his 9). [S repo]
- **Runner (Leonard vs Hagler, Conn early).** Not a real style but a phase: movement used as flight. Forum and coaching pieces say judges should score excessive retreat, and cutting the ring is the standard answer (Louis vs Conn). [S, ?]

## Slugger subs
- **One-shot puncher (Wilder, Foreman, Lemieux, Klitschko Vitali).** Plants the feet, few punches, hunts one big shot, low volume. [M: Wikipedia, Boxing Explainers] Weak: one dimension. Parker beat Wilder by moving and denying the right hand; Lemieux's same entry was read by Saunders. [S Boxing Explainers]
- **Come-forward brawler / trader (Gatti, Provodnikov, Ward, Mayorga, Maidana).** Walks through shots, hooks to head and body, trades, takes damage to give it. Wikipedia: slow and predictable, tires if no early KO, beaten by faster counter-punchers and mobile jabbers. [S] Maidana was more tactical (jab, feints, awkward angles) than a pure mauler. [S, ?]
- **Pressure power (Beterbiev, Golovkin, Hagler, Tszyu).** Walks you down, cuts the ring, breaks the guard, heavy hands, steady output not a swing-for-the-fence. Skillr: pressure / sniper / starter ("one power shot at a time"). Troy Isley adds: smart pressure is feet, jab, feints, and wait when the man is hurt. [M: repo, Boxing Wiki, Wikipedia]
- **Counter-slugger (Wilder at times, Foreman vs Moorer).** Waits, throws one counter with full weight. Wilder and Davis are the headline cases. Davis is called a slugger by one list but counters his way into the shot (Romero, Garcia). [S Boxing Explainers]
- **Explosive entry puncher (Romero, Lemieux, Tyson early).** Closes in a burst behind a right hand. Great when it lands, but easy to time once read (Romero stopped in 6 by Davis). [S]

## Swarmer subs
- **Peekaboo bob-and-weave (Tyson, Patterson, Frazier, Vinny Paz, Bradley).** Gloves at the cheeks, constant head movement, walks in, short hooks and uppercuts. Wikipedia describes it as moving forward as a counter-puncher: charge to provoke, slip, hit the opening. [S Wikipedia, Teddy Atlas / D'Amato sources agree on the head-movement core] Weak to reach and an outside fighter; critics said an efficient attack could not come from it. [S, a low-authority quote]
- **Volume grinder (Hatton, Zepeda, Pacquiao, Gonzalez).** Forward, steady 60-80 a round, body and head, breaks you down. Pacquiao has speed and angles beyond a plain swarmer. Cardio is the price. [M: Boxing Wiki, Wikipedia]
- **Body puncher / cutter-off (Chavez, Golovkin, Duran).** Chavez and GGG are the "gold standards" of pressure; the body work slows legs over time. [M: Boxing Wiki, Boxing Explainers]
- **Crowding infighter (Marciano, Frazier, LaMotta, Bobo Olson).** Crouches, absorbs to get inside, hooks. Needs a chin; sluggers beat pressure fighters. [S Wikipedia]
- **Explosive first-step swarmer (Teofimo Lopez vs Commey, Tyson).** Two-round blitz style; gas risk if it fails. [S Boxing Explainers, ?]

## Counter-puncher subs
- **Shoulder roll / Philly shell (Mayweather, Toney, Whitaker partly).** Shell takes the first shot, the check hook and the counter come off the roll. Toney: "needs you to throw first". [M: FightCamp, Boxing Explainers]
- **Pull counter / timing counter (Marquez, Hopkins, Canelo).** Reads the pattern, gives a target, sets a 3-fight read. Marquez countered space, steps and feints, not only punches (Bad Left Hook). Canelo wants you to lead and uses feints and head movement to pull you into range. [S Boxing Explainers, S TSS]
- **Trap-setter, low-hands baiter (Martinez, Davis, Pep, Roy Jones).** Skillr on Martinez: low open guard keeps shoulders loose and punches relaxed, leans on the front foot to look vulnerable, rotates off the central line so his left comes in and stays out of counter range. Risk: low guard eats a clean shot if he is caught. [S Skillr summary] Davis: patient, gives ground, times the entry (Romero, Garcia). [S Boxing Explainers]
- **Awkward / rhythm breaker (Hamed, Augustus, Whittaker, Hamed-like).** No patterns, exaggerated body movement, odd angles, unreadable rhythm. Wikipedia lists Hamed as counter and switch-hitter and out-boxer. [S Boxing Explainers; Wikipedia]
- **Veteran spoiler (Hopkins, Archie Moore, Maxim).** Hopkins is a counter-puncher on Wikipedia and a ring-reading spoiler on Boxing Explainers: makes you fight his fight by the middle rounds. [S]
- **Cuban / sniper counter (Rigondeaux, Lara).** See out-boxer subs; counters are the plan, volume is the weakness. [M]
- Counter weakness: poor reflexes or speed leave him hurt badly; plain straights down the middle hit even Marquez; low output loses rounds. [S Wikipedia, S TSS, S Boxing Explainers]

## Boxer-puncher subs
- **Complete boxer (Leonard, Usyk, Bivol, Ennis, Ward).** Fights at any range, adjusts mid-fight, jab controls, power when the range closes. Wikipedia: master of none is the risk; rock-paper-scissors doesn't work against him. [M: Wikipedia, Boxing Explainers]
- **Power boxer-puncher (Hearns, Arguello, Jones, Cotto, Trinidad, Inoue).** Starts boxing and ends with a big finisher; Inoue is patient sniper then explosive (fighters_a.md). [S Wikipedia list, S repo]
- **Switch-hitter (Crawford, Hagler, Hamed, Ward, Mayweather early).** Two stances, flips to read reactions. Wikipedia keeps it as its own "category", separate from style. [S]
- **Angle fighter (Lomachenko, Pacquiao-like, Whitaker).** Pivots and steps off the line, not a block-based defense; see the out-volume entry. [M]
- **Feint master (Lomachenko matador feints, Ali, Pep, Rigondeaux).** Feints to draw and freeze. No page lists "feint master" as a style; it is a trait. [?]
- **Tall boxer-puncher (Lewis, Klitschko, Fury, Fundora).** Long jab then right hand. Fundora is tall and volume, with defensive lapses (Mendoza). [S Boxing Explainers]

## The empty corners (out-volume, out-volume-counter, pressure-counter)
- **Out-volume (out / volume / starter).** Real examples: Lomachenko (about 65 thrown a round at Linares), Whitaker (sources disagree on his volume; Jackson says he threw many), many Soviet amateurs (skillr.md), Haney, Stevenson at times (he can also be the opposite). How: moves, doubles and triples the jab, pivots off it, feints, throws all night with a big tank. [S ESPN, S repo, ?]
- **Out-volume-counter (out / volume / counter).** Martinez is Skillr's example: loose snappy shots from a low guard keep you out and bait you, then a sudden hard counter. Also Pep and Whitaker (slips, then counters with volume), Hamed, Davis at times. [S Skillr, ?]
- **Pressure-counter (forward / counter).** Two kinds:
  - Sniper: walks in behind a high guard or bob-and-weave, throws little, wants the one perfect shot, catches you when you try to back him up. Real examples: Tyson/Patterson peekaboo (Wikipedia: moving forward as counter-puncher), Marquez (stays at close range keeping pressure and counters), Wilder at times. [S Wikipedia, S Skillr]
  - Volume: forward volume with tight defense, the volume baits you, then the counter. Real examples: Duran (listed counter on Wikipedia), Chavez Sr. (listed counter and swarmer), Hagler, Hatton? [S Wikipedia list; the volume kind is Skillr's, fighter names are mine, ?]
  - Fits with the fact that pressure denies a counter-puncher his openings if the pressure volume is high (Boxing Wiki/Evolve), so a real pressure-counter must be defensively tight. [M]

## Our 9 specials vs the real sub-types (match / missing, missing ranked)
Matches (existing special -> real sub-type):
- **Jab-and-grab (out-boxer)** -> ring general / jabber / tall clinching jabber (Lewis, Klitschko, Holmes). Good match. [M]
- **Pressure boxer (slugger)** -> pressure power (Golovkin, Beterbiev, Hagler). Good match. [M]
- **Volume puncher (swarmer)** -> volume grinder (Hatton, Zepeda, Pacquiao). Good match. [M]
- **Body snatcher (swarmer)** -> body puncher (Chavez, GGG). Good match. [M]
- **Awkward (counter)** -> awkward / rhythm breaker (Hamed, Augustus, Whittaker). Good match. [S]
- **Veteran spoiler (counter)** -> veteran spoiler (Hopkins, Moore, Maxim). Fine, though Hopkins is also the pull-counter ring general. [S]
- **Angle fighter (boxer-puncher)** -> angle fighter (Lomachenko, Whitaker, Pacquiao-ish). Good. It is also the nearest thing to out-volume. [M]
- **Switch-hitter (boxer-puncher)** -> a trick, not a real style bucket (Wikipedia gives it its own category). Real, but narrower than the other sub-types. [S]
- **Feint master (boxer-puncher)** -> a trait more than a recognized sub-type. Real, but least named. [?]

Missing, ranked by how common / recognizable (my judgement):
1. **Shell / roll defensive counter** (Mayweather, Toney, Whitaker, Stevenson). Very named. Game has the Philly guard and counter base, but no special tied to the "makes you miss, low output, steers you" play. [M]
2. **Out-volume Soviet / Loma type**. A famous empty corner; angle fighter is the closest. [M]
3. **Peekaboo pressure-counter** (Tyson, Frazier, Patterson, Paz). Very recognizable; game has the peekaboo guard but it is a guard, not a sub. [M]
4. **Come-forward brawler / trader** (Gatti, Provodnikov, Ward, Maidana, Mayorga). The slugger base has one special (pressure boxer) and the other face (trader that takes to give) is missing. [M]
5. **Low-hand baiter / out-volume-counter** (Martinez, Davis, Hamed, Roy Jones, Pep). [S, ?]
6. **Long-guard tall jabber** (Klitschko, Fury, Bivol). Recognized as a type of tall boxer; close to jab-and-grab but different in kind (posts the arm, blocks the jab, less clinch). [M]
7. **Cuban low-output sniper counter mover** (Rigondeaux, Lara, Scull). Real, but the "boring" flaw is judges and it is niche. [M]
8. **Power boxer-puncher** (Hearns, Arguello, Cotto, Inoue). Common, but sits inside the base boxer-puncher already. [?]
9. **One-shot sniper vs explosive entry puncher** (Wilder, Lemieux, Romero). Also close to base slugger. [?]

## For Last Bell
- **Out-boxer has only one sub, but the type has the most real variety.** Build two more first: the **out-volume boxer** (Soviet/Lomachenko type, fills the empty corner, high output + angle pivots, big tank) and the **slick shell boxer** (Mayweather/Whitaker/Stevenson: low risk, steers, spurts). - **Long-guard boxer should be a sub, but a narrow one.** It is easy to explain (posts the lead hand, jab and traffic) and longguard.md already lists mechanics, but real long-guard use is a moment, not a style, so make it a gear (Ed's Oct 20 call) on jab-and-grab and the tall boxer-puncher first, a standalone sub third. [?]
- **Cheapest fill for the pressure-counter corner:** a peekaboo sub on the swarmer ("Tyson walks forward, slips, counters"). The game already has the guard and the brain's modes; this is mostly a trait change, not a new engine mechanic. [?]
- **Slugger needs a second face:** brawler/trader (comes in, takes, trades) vs the one-shot waiter. Right now only pressure boxer exists, which is the quiet one. [?]
- **Counter base needs the low-hand baiter** (Martinez/Davis): it is where the f2 bait mechanic already lives, so the out-volume-counter corner may be reachable by giving a counter special more volume and the bait. [?]
- **Keep switch-hitter and feint master as perks, not styles,** since sources treat them as extra categories or traits. [S, ?]
- **Balance flag:** low-output movers (Stevenson, Rigondeaux) lose to the scorecard, and slick shell boxers lose to body pressure (fighters_a.md). If we add a shell boxer, keep that flaw; the out-boxer base is already the low styles at 46.8% in the latest check. [S repo]
- **Don't add a "runner" sub.** It is a phase and a criticism, not a style, and the game's refs and judges would need new rules. [?]
