## 1. Eddy Reynoso (Canelo)

### Philosophy
- Counterpunch plus cutting the ring. His own summary of Canelo: "cut off the ring and hit on the counter." [Sportskeeda, Bivol preview](https://www.sportskeeda.com/pro-boxing/news-boxing-news-eddy-reynoso-canelo-alvarez-vs-dmitry-bivol)
- Chose to build counterpunching in Canelo, unusual for a Mexican pressure style. [TSS profile](https://tss.ib.tv/boxing/eddy-reynoso-is-the-tss-2020-trainer-of-the-year)
- Body hook is identity: "if you don't know how to land a good hook to the body, then you're not a Mexican fighter." Offense should "complement our defense." [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/32591)
- Teacher framing: "Be a professor that teaches boxing" (from his manager Rafael Mendoza). [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/32591)

### Reading opponents / tells
- Nothing documented on hurt, tired or bluff tells. Unverified.
- ESPN feature describes body-liver coaching in camp, not a read. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/32552636/canelo-alvarez-mystical-man-quest-immortality)

### Traps and baiting
- Saunders: planned counter. When Saunders leaned in with his left, Canelo steps back and throws the uppercut. Drilled in camp "repetition after repetition." [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/32591)
- Pattern: pick one known lean/reach habit, build one scripted answer, drill it.

### Corner / adjusting
- No corner audio found. Unverified.
- GGG 1 (draw): his post-fight diagnosis was output, "needed 40 more punches" and more aggression to push Golovkin back. For the rematch he said they changed the whole game plan. [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/pro-boxing/news-canelo-alvarez-s-trainer-eddy-reynoso-recalls-strategy-first-fight-ggg)
- Bivol (loss): said they "didn't lose the fight, just some rounds"; stamina not the cause. Moving to 175 "didn't give us very good results." [3Kings](https://3kingsboxing.com/eddy-reynoso-believes-canelo-beats-bivol-rematch)

### Finishing vs surviving
- Unverified. Pre-Crawford he promised a KO; Canelo lost that fight (Sept 2025), so treat the claim as bluster. [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/crawford-has-a-secret-plan-but-canelo-will-knock-him-out-promises-eddy-reynoso)

### Fixing flaws
- Method is repetition of one scripted pattern. No timeline found.

### Size
- Canelo went up to 175 vs Bivol and it did not work (above). Taller/longer men (Bivol, Crawford-style movers) beat him on distance; Reynoso's fix was never documented. Unverified.

## 2. Anatoly Lomachenko (Vasyl, Usyk)

### Philosophy
- Footwork first, then fundamentals, then borrowed skills from other sports. Goal: read when the opponent will be out of position and move to where he is going. [SI](https://www.si.com/boxing/2018/05/09/vasiliy-lomachenko-boxing-footwork-dancing-jorge-linares)
- Vasyl: "All the power, all the coordination, all my victories come out of my footwork." [SI](https://www.si.com/boxing/2018/05/09/vasiliy-lomachenko-boxing-footwork-dancing-jorge-linares)
- Dance (Hopak) and gymnastics before boxing, per SI.
- Punch output is used to step around (Malignaggi's read, not Anatoly's): "using his punch output to step around." [SI](https://www.si.com/boxing/2018/05/09/vasiliy-lomachenko-boxing-footwork-dancing-jorge-linares)

### Reading opponents
- Anatoly's own tells (hurt, tired, bluff): none found. Unverified.
- Cognitive side: number grids, reaction timer, maths while fatigued, run by a psychologist. Aim is decisions under fatigue. [ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/21681715/training-just-piece-puzzle-vasiliy-lomachenko-lomachenko-vs-rigondeaux-espn), [Sky](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/11526433/the-vasyl-lomachenko-experiment)

### Traps / baiting
- Specific traps: none documented by Anatoly. Unverified.
- General: angle creation as the trap. Step off the line while punching so the counter has nothing to hit.

### Corner
- No quotes found. Unverified. Later chief second for Usyk (2021). [Bad Left Hook](https://www.badlefthook.com/2021/6/30/22557026/oleksandr-usyk-reunites-trainer-anatoly-lomachenko-next-fight-anthony-joshua-boxing-news-2021)
- Usyk-Joshua 2 movement reports (circle to own right, keep the lead foot outside) are fan/blog analysis, not tied to Anatoly. Unverified.

### Finishing / surviving
- Unverified.

### Teaching
- Heavy volume: sparring of 15 four-minute rounds, partners rotating every three rounds, hand-wrap chips recording every punch; breath-hold swims; juggling, tennis, skating. [ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/21681715/training-just-piece-puzzle-vasiliy-lomachenko-lomachenko-vs-rigondeaux-espn)
- Started as a child; years, not weeks.

### Size
- Unverified from Anatoly. (Inference) Lateral angles beat reach by making the tall man turn to find you.

## 3. Derrick James (Spence, Charlo, Ryan Garcia)

### Philosophy
- Fundamentals over glamour: "basic skills and technique beats glamor every day of the week." [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/derrick-james-basic-skills-technique-beats-glamor-every-day-week)
- The jab does several jobs: sets up shots, creates distance, keeps the man occupied; "does more than any other punch." [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/31239)
- Being on the ropes is not automatically defense; it can be a setup, and he drills it so the fighter is comfortable there. [PBC Fundamentals](https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/31239)
- Pushed Spence to throw more jabs and body shots early in the partnership; footwork, head movement and defense improved. [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/errol-spence-derrick-james-match-made-dallas)

### Traps

James said he gave multiple plans and conceded BoMac was "the better guy" with a "great game plan." Spence also reported a bad camp. [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/derrick-james-says-errol-spence-being-weight-drained-wasnt-issue-against-crawford), [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/terence-crawford-believes-derrick-james-great-coach-bad-job-on-july-29th--177301)

- Mikey Garcia: much bigger Spence could not finish him. Unverified why.

### Fixing flaws

## 4. Shingo Inoue (Naoya)

### Philosophy
- "Naoya is no genius." Same advice given since he was a boy; weaknesses drilled until fixed. [Nippon.com / Sankei](https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-topics/g00734/the-monster-shaking-up-the-world-of-boxing.html), [Sankei](https://material.human.sankei.co.jp/news/2020/20200304.html)
- Core drills named: pull the gloves back fast after punching; throw combinations while repositioning so the next counter misses. [Nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-topics/g00734/the-monster-shaking-up-the-world-of-boxing.html)
- Started Naoya at about five; very strict. [Ring](https://www.ringtv.com/?p=516335)
- Camp volume: 116 sparring rounds for Tapales (Dec 2023) vs 70-80 for the previous 2-3 fights. [Boxing News Japan](https://boxingnews.jp/?p=104888)

### Reading opponents
- Father's tells: none found. Unverified.

### Traps / baiting (Inoue's style, analysts, not Shingo)
- Lift the guard with feints or head shots, then go to the body (left uppercut to the solar plexus, hook to the liver). Opponents guard the body more by the middle rounds, which opens the head. [Evolve breakdown](https://evolve-mma.com/blog/breaking-down-naoya-inoues-style-of-fighting/), [Honour & Glory](https://www.honourandglory.co.uk/learn/boxer-styles/naoya-inoue) (blog-level sources).
- Cardenas (May 2025): Inoue dropped in round 2 by a counter left hook while trying to return aggression; his fix: "make sure not to take that punch again." Won R8 TKO. [Ring](https://ringmagazine.com/en/news/naoya-inoue-survives-knockdown-war-with-ramon-cardenas-to-score-8th-round-tko-in-fight-of-the-year-contender)
- Nery (May 2024): dropped R1, rallied, stopped R6. Used power jab and one-twos to rebuild range after the knockdown. [Ring](https://www.ringtv.com/669563-naoya-inoue-survives-knockdown-kos-luis-nery-in-six-retains-undisputed-122-pound-championship/)

### Corner
- Quotes: none found. Unverified.

### Finishing vs surviving
- Naoya asked his father and gym chairman Ohashi to tell him to retire if they see decline (he would not judge it himself). [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/boxing/articles/inoue_tells_team_to_stop_him_when_decline_comes/s1_17349_44279804)
- Survival from the Nery and Cardenas knockdowns: get up, calm, no panic (his own words in the Cardenas piece).

### Fixing flaws
- Years of repetition of one note until it is automatic.

### Size
- Moving up from 112 to 122 and beyond: kept the same method; body work and feints cover for the weight gap. Shingo's own words: unverified.

## 7. Rob McCracken (Joshua; GB amateurs)

### Philosophy
- "If you've got the height and reach, like Josh, you must use it; the minute you don't, you're open to quick powerful hooks off these heavyweights." (reported Guardian/PA wording via [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/?p=1068154)).
- Team approach: outside specialists on pads and footwork. [Sporting Life](https://www.sportinglife.com/boxing/news/joshua-stands-by-mccracken/174288)

- The documented case: Ruiz 1, round 3. He saw Joshua "glazy-eyed" from the R3 knockdown and said "I knew he was concussed and I'm trying to get him through a few more rounds, one round at a time, and see where he's at." Later: he meant "hurt," he is "not a doctor." [Bad Left Hook](https://www.badlefthook.com/2019/9/12/20862342/rob-mccracken-admits-he-knew-anthony-joshua-was-concussed-during-andy-ruiz-fight), [World Boxing News](https://www.worldboxingnews.net/2019/09/12/anthony-joshua-coach-concussion-deadly/)
- Tell used: eyes (glazy), not hands or legs.

### Traps
- None documented. Unverified.

### Corner
- Ruiz 1: criticised (Lennox Lewis and others) for Joshua not using reach and brawling in the pocket. [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/?p=1068154)
- Rematch (Dec 2019) was a boxing, jab-and-distance win (from my own knowledge, not these sources; verify).

### Finishing vs surviving
- "Survive and see where he's at" is his approach to a hurt fighter: keep him in, round by round, hope the head clears. Criticised as dangerous.

### Fixing flaws

### Size
- Tall, long heavyweight: the rule is use the jab and range. Joshua's loss came from abandoning it.

## 8. Brian "BoMac" McIntyre (Crawford)

### Philosophy
- Crawford + trio of trainers speak in unison in the corner (Herring noticed this in 2017 sparring). [ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/29801051/jamel-herring-needed-one-big-change-thrive-ring-true-team)

### Reading opponents (best-documented trainer here)
- Spence "never changed his fight style," "basic," starts every fight with the jab, hardly brings his feet when he goes to the body, legs too wide so he reaches and comes up short. [Bad Left Hook](https://www.badlefthook.com/2023/7/31/23814000/brian-mcintyre-breaks-down-why-terence-crawford-dominated-basic-errol-spence-boxing-news-2023), [Boxing Social](https://boxing-social.com/news/crawford-trainer-reveals-how-to-beat-spence/)
- After round one: "he was slow as sh*t," movement and hands lagging. [Boxing Scene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/terence-crawford-trainer-if-bud-another-minute-he-ve-stopped-spence-second-round)
- Hurt-man read: after the R2 knockdown (20 seconds left) he believed Spence was "more hurt than he let on" and "would have folded" with another minute. [Boxing Scene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/terence-crawford-trainer-if-bud-another-minute-he-ve-stopped-spence-second-round)
- Tell used: slowing feet and hands after the first exchange, and lunging reach.

### Traps / baiting
- Counter jab over the top: "This motherfucker is not fast as you. Any chance you get to counter over the top, make it happen." Built on Spence's lunging straight left, which leaves a brief window out of position. Crawford lived in the southpaw stance against him (Ring analysis). [Boxing Scene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/terence-crawford-trainer-if-bud-another-minute-he-ve-stopped-spence-second-round), [Ring](https://www.ringtv.com/?p=662708)
- Only attack the double jab near the ropes; stay mobile, don't stand in the middle. [Bad Left Hook](https://www.badlefthook.com/2023/7/31/23814000/brian-mcintyre-breaks-down-why-terence-crawford-dominated-basic-errol-spence-boxing-news-2023)
- Prep: a firmer jab built in camp to neutralize Spence's.
- First round likely lost on purpose or accepted: "The first couple rounds gonna be real tough," then Crawford gets range and times him, "like a home run." [Boxing Scene](https://www.boxingscene.com/bomac-expects-game-spence-first-couple-rounds-gonna-real-tough--175798)

### Corner
- Documented instruction above (R2). One voice, one clear order. More corner audio: not found.

### Finishing
- Would have stopped Spence in R2 with a minute left (his claim). Crawford "flash knockdown."

### Fixing flaws / exploiting
- Exploited a man who never adapts, slow counters, feet slower than hands. "Once a fighter's feet are slower than their hands, they'll always come up short." [FightHype via Boxing Scene piece]

### Size
- Crawford (smaller) beat the bigger Spence by timing the reach and pulling him onto a counter. Plan ignored size, played to timing.

## For Last Bell

Sourced rules are tagged with a trainer. (inference) rules are my design reading, not a documented quote. Numbers are starting values for sim tuning, not facts.

1. If a man lunges with his lead (jab or straight) and leaves his feet behind, then counter over the top on the very next lunge; raise counter-window odds about +50% for the next 3 exchanges once the habit has shown twice. Tag: BoMac (Spence), Reynoso (Saunders).
2. If a man's first exchange tires or slows him (hands and feet visibly late, 15-25% slower than round start), then the AI corner calls "he is slow, counter, don't chase." Tag: BoMac.
3. If a man gets dropped and rises showing no wobble but his feet get slower and wider, treat him as hurt-bluffing, not fine. Press for 20-30 seconds with hard counters, not wild volleys. Tag: BoMac ("more hurt than he let on"); (inference) on feet/hands cues.
4. A vet reads a bluff by eyes first, then legs: glazy/slow-reacting eyes after a knockdown (a late block or no slip), plus reduced footwork, means hurt. Make a "glazy" flag from reaction delay (+0.2-0.3 s) in the first 2 punches after the hit. Tag: McCracken.
5. If your own fighter is hurt: get through the round, do not trade. Move off the line, tuck chin, clinch if wobbled (brace on impact: chin down, forehead out). The corner's first call is "breathe, calm" at the bell. Tag: Steward, Davison, McCracken.
6. If hurt and the corner judges the head is not clearing, the "survive round by round" rule has a limit: allow at most ~2 more rounds of "get him through" before the corner pushes for a stoppage-protect or a gamble. Tag: McCracken (and the criticism of him).
7. A tired man's hands drop and his output falls; if the opponent's output drops more than ~25% from round 2 average and his guard hand sags, raise the right-hand lead and head-shot weight. (inference); BoMac's "hands and feet lagging" is the closest documented tell.
8. A body-hurt man clamps his elbows and the head opens; after two clean body hits in 90 seconds, favour the head (hook or uppercut over the guard) at ~60% of the shots. Tag: Inoue (guard-lift/body-first, then head), Reynoso (body hook). Elbow-clamp tell itself: unverified, (inference).
9. Bait the guard: throw a head feint or head shot to lift the guard, then go to the body (left hook to liver, uppercut to solar plexus). Use it when the opponent's guard answers the head more than 70% of feints. Tag: Inoue.
10. By the middle rounds (rounds 5-8) men guard the body harder; open the head instead of banging the same body shot. Tag: Inoue (analyst claim).
11. Do not repeat a failed plan: if the plan's key punch has landed under 10% over 2 rounds, the AI corner switches the plan (angle, range, body) rather than repeating "step around." Tag: Derrick James (Crawford fight failure), BoMac.
12. Build one scripted counter per opponent habit and run it instead of free play. Odds the brain picks the scripted counter on a trigger: high (60%+), falls if it misses twice. Tag: Reynoso (Saunders uppercut), BoMac.
13. Tall/long man: use the jab to hold distance; if he drops the jab and fights in the pocket, he takes more hooks. Penalise a long man who stays inside (more hits taken) unless the opponent is clearly slower. Tag: McCracken, Steward, Davison.
14. Short or smaller man: win the first two rounds' feel by taking range timing, then counter. Allow him to lose round 1 (accept it) if the plan is timing a long man. Tag: BoMac.
15. Lomachenko-style angle: after a combination, step off to an angle instead of backing straight. Treat any combo ending in a lateral step as protected from the next counter (~-30% chance of being countered). Tag: Anatoly/Vasyl (footwork principle); numbers (inference).
16. Pull gloves back fast after punching. Brain "recovery" stat sets how soon the guard is back; a slow recoverer eats more counters. Tag: Shingo Inoue.
17. Easy flaws to exploit in weaker fighters: never changes style (Spence per BoMac), bounces on the spot (Wilder per Davison), reaches because stance is wide, lunges with one hand, leaves the body open after the jab. The brain scouts 2 rounds then locks one of these as the target. Tag: BoMac, Davison.
18. Never try a new tactic on the night: a fighter's stat for a skill used in the fight is capped by what was drilled. If the corner shouts a move the fighter has not drilled, success drops (e.g., -30%). Tag: Davison.
19. Corner talk: one voice, 1-2 instructions per break, one key note + one breath cue. Penalise a corner that gives 3+ notes or "too many voices" (-10 to -20% follow-through). Tag: BoMac (unison trio), Steward/John Fury dispute, Davison. Counts unverified.
20. After a knockdown against, do not chase revenge: the first reply is a clean jab/counter, not a haymaker. Reduce the volley chance in the 10 seconds after standing (Inoue after Cardenas: "not take that punch again"). Tag: Inoue, Steward.
