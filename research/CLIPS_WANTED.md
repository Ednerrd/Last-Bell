## What makes a clip useful
- **Decisions, not technique**: "when he does X, you do Y, because Z". The engine/animation handles form.
- Good titles: "how to beat", "when", "why", "vs". Usually skip: "fix", "drill", "workout", "power", conditioning, bag work.
- Breakdowns of real fights beat pad-work demos. A named fighter ("like Crawford") ties the idea to a style.
- 5 good transcripts beat 25 random ones.

## Wanted, in order
1. **Real corner audio between rounds** (mic'd-up trainers, "listen to the corner of X vs Y"). For trainers + corners: what they say first, how blunt, the one adjustment in 60 s. Top priority: can't be researched from text.
2. **Tall vs short** (short man getting inside, tall man keeping him off, Fundora breakdowns). For size. Gap with no data: **leaning on a man to tire him**.
3. **Surviving hurt**: clinch, run, cover, when to fire back. For the hurt brain.
4. **Fight breakdowns**: "why X beat Y", the adjustment that won it (Lee Wylie style). For between-round adjustments.
5. **Jab defense**: how pros shut down the jab. Our jab connect ~27% vs real ~17%.
