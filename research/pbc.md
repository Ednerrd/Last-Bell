## G1. Shawn Porter on Adrian Granados (Nov 2017, UD 12)

**The plan.** Granados: rough, durable, pressure, takes head shots. Camp was mostly sparring with men told to walk straight at him. Plan: speed, the jab to control him, **the body to slow a man who takes it upstairs**, and **make it a 12-round fight because Granados had never gone 12**: take him into the 11th-12th and stop him late.

**What he did, round by round (his reasons)**
- **The ropes are fire** (his dad's rule since childhood): he spins off them; he's only on the ropes when he chooses to be, and leaves when he's ready.
- **Up-down-up with the jab** (head, body, head) set up the overhand right.
- **Smother** a pressure man who wants in-and-out: don't let him be comfortable, push him to the ropes, then go to work.
- **Touch the side, the elbow follows, go up the middle**: a hook to the body opens the uppercut / right down the centre.
- **Change the dynamic:** when the other man taunts "come on", he refuses. "You can't get me out of my game."
- **Don't pull straight back with your hands down:** he punished Granados every time with the overhand right, and admits he did it himself too (and fell off balance).
- **Posturing:** walking up on him looking calm, showing "you can't touch me": tells him you're confident and keeps him off.
- **"Flowing":** got caught with an uppercut after staying too long, took it and came straight back with right-hook. Stay in the rhythm when hit.
- **Stops bouncing = ready to hit.** Round 7 the corner said stop moving so much, pivot, time him. Corner's other calls: R4 "just touch him with the jab, don't be so aggressive"; R6 "keep boxing, it's working, we'll wear him down and get him late"; R7 "don't pressure him so much, use the middle of the ring".
- **His big mistake:** reaching. Jabs and rights thrown from out of range because he wanted to hurt him ("punches I wanted to hit him with, not thinking"); speed in his head all night, so he never sat down on the punches.
- **Both hands low on the other man** = you never know which is coming; awkward, makes you hesitate.
- **Foot feint:** pick up the front foot and put it down to one side, he looks that way, pop back in.

**The hurt hand (the part we don't have)**
- Hurt his **left (jab) hand** around rounds 6-8. Not every punch hurt, only some, so he kept jabbing out of instinct and kept getting the sharp pain. You can see it: **the hand sits far lower than his normal guard.**
- Then: **more right hands**, more defense, pivots to the right first; the pace dropped. In the 10th he threw a hook, really hurt it, and **clinched** right after so he couldn't be hit.
- **Between 11 and 12 he told his dad "I'm done."** Dad: you can't stop, you lose the belt; hands up, use your feet, don't let him touch you. So the last rounds: move, hands high, change levels, smother, push off rather than hold, run the clock. The ref didn't see the exchange.
- The plan flipped: from "stop him late" to "survive and win on the cards".
- Granados, frustrated he couldn't catch a mover, kept taunting him in; Porter ignored it. Granados blew up at the decision (his third close loss to a big name) and later called to apologize.

### For Last Bell (not built)
1. **The injured hand:** a damaged hand (a hard landed shot, a rare roll, more for punchers) makes him throw it less, hold it lower, and lean on the other hand; the other man can spot the low hand (like `Look.elbows`). A whole new damage zone: big, park it for a design pass.
2. **The fighter wants out; the corner says no:** a late quit request the corner refuses (or accepts: the towel). Pairs with the towel in `toprank.md` and the "show me or I stop it" corners in `corners_video.md` batch 6.
3. **"Take him past where he's been":** a plan vs a man with no 12-round experience: box, bank the body, stop him in 10-12. An AI corner plan keyed off his record.
4. **The taunt that doesn't work:** a frustrated pressure man calls him in; a disciplined boxer ignores it, a hot-headed one bites. Brain `emo.frus` + discipline/level.
5. **Reaching as a mistake:** a man who wants to hurt him throws from out of range: lower connect, less power. Fits `BR.flaw` or `emo` (angry = reaches).
6. **Pulling straight back with the hands down** is a hole the overhand right punishes: a defensive flaw for low levels.
7. **The ropes are fire:** a spin-off-the-ropes skill (Porter's whole life); compare `tests/ropes.js` time on the ropes by style.
