## 1. Freddie Roach

### Philosophy / system
- Mitt work as fight simulation. He moves around the ring, plays the opponent's style, and calls the punches the fighter must throw. Ring-reading is drilled by reacting to what the mitts do. https://philstar.com/sports/2014/11/23/1394908/working-mitts-roach
- Studies tape for patterns and weaknesses, builds the plan around one exploitable habit, then rehearses it on the mitts. https://philstar.com/sports/2014/11/23/1394908/working-mitts-roach
- Speed is the equaliser. He said Pacquiao's speed offsets De La Hoya's size. https://www.philstar.com/sports/2008/10/11/406440/roach-gives-pacman-90-percent-prevail/amp/
- Learned under Eddie Futch (profile paraphrase, weak source). Unverified as to specifics.
- Pacquiao feedback loop: the fighter changed moves on the mitts, Roach kept what worked. https://philstar.com/sports/2014/11/23/1394908/working-mitts-roach

### Reading an opponent
- Hatton: Roach spotted that Hatton cocked or pumped his hands before throwing, and put his chin up. Every time he cocked the left hook he was open to the southpaw's short right hook. Roach said they drilled it every day. https://gmanetwork.com/news/sports/content/160145/roach-plan-solution-to-hatton-s-brawler-style/story and https://www.philstar.com/sports/2009/05/04/463752/manny-reveals-secret-weapon/amp/
- De La Hoya: Roach reviewed the 2001 Whitaker fight and said De La Hoya had not improved; age made reflexes slower. https://tss.ib.tv/boxing/boxing-articles-2008/6345-coach-roach-says-he-knows-odlhs-weakness
- Mayweather 2015: Roach claimed "I don't think his legs are there anymore" and "he never hurt Oscar." Pre-fight talk, not a proven tell. https://www.pressreader.com/philippines/philippine-daily-inquirer-1109/20150429/281925951564100
- Roach on Mayweather: "won't counter until you stop. Some fighters will just keep throwing at him. My fighter shouldn't." Also said the shoulder roll's right-hand counter can be exploited by a southpaw. https://www.spin.ph/boxing/freddie-roach-on-mayweather-jr-i-don (summary only)
- Hurt / tired / bluffing tells: not documented. Unverified.

### Traps and baiting
- Hatton: the trap was the habit itself (cocking the hook). Wait for the cock, fire the right hook over it. Hatton went down twice in round 1. https://www.philstar.com/sports/2009/05/04/463752/manny-reveals-secret-weapon/amp/
- Pacquiao's "over the jab" right hook was reported as a Roach-endorsed punch thrown as the man expects the left. Source conflict about which punch ended Hatton (left hook per ESPN/Sky). Treat as partly unverified. https://www.rappler.com/sports/2799-top-10-boxing-tricks-of-manny-pacquiao/
- Margarito fight: keep away from the ropes and from standing in front of the bigger man. https://gmanetwork.com/news/sports/content/202437/roach-reminds-ward-pacquiao-to-fight-smart/story

### Corner work
- Few seconds, few words. Roach said he only needs the seconds before the bell. One voice only: "Freddie's is the one voice I will listen to between rounds" (Pacquiao). Roach spoke so quietly it could not be heard five feet away. https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/25765077/pacquiao-seeks-many-voices-run-bout
- Documented cue (Jorge Julio): "double-jab, then the left straight." https://africa.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/25765077/pacquiao-seeks-many-voices-run-bout
- Marquez IV, after the round-3 knockdown: told Pacquiao to go under the overhand right, not pull away (Wikipedia summary, no verbatim). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manny_Pacquiao_vs._Juan_Manuel_M%C3%A1rquez_IV
- Failure mode: too many voices. Roach admitted the corner was a mess at times. https://www.badlefthook.com/2017/7/6/15928332/roach-admits-to-disarray-in-pacquiao-corner
- De La Hoya vs Mayweather 2007: Roach's plan was high-volume jabbing; De La Hoya dropped it mid-fight and lost. Roach said he could have won by keeping to it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_De_La_Hoya_vs._Floyd_Mayweather_Jr.

### Finishing vs surviving
- Horn fight, round 9: Pacquiao had Horn hurt and could not finish. Roach: "If you give me another one of those [rounds], this fight is over." Pacquiao was spent in round 10. Finishing costs gas; a hurt man who survives can steal the rounds back. https://sports.inquirer.net/254959/last-one-pacquiao-roach
- "There's no quit in real fighters" (about himself). Rigondeaux "too small, a bit too old." https://www.badlefthook.com/2017/12/13/16771758/roach-on-lomachenko-rigondeaux-theres-no-quit-in-real-fighters
- Margarito: Roach said Garcia's corner may have ruined his career by not stopping it. https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/margaritos-trainer-fight-changed-round-four

### Fixing flaws
- Not documented in detail. Method is mitts plus the sim. Time to fix: unverified.

### Size
- "Size don't win fights, skill does." "I don't think height means anything. It's not a basketball game." Said of Margarito, Clottey, Algieri, De La Hoya. https://philstar.com/sports/2010/10/18/621630/roach-size-wont-matter and https://gmanetwork.com/news/sports/boxing/388608/roach-downplays-algieri-s-height-advantage-over-pacquiao-it-s-not-basketball/story

## 2. Floyd Mayweather Sr.

### Philosophy / system
- Defense first. Worked with his son only on defense in the first days. Getting hit in the head day after day shortens a career and dulls thinking. Make the man hard to hit, then teach how to hit with little risk (secondhand summary). https://www.boxinginsider.com/headlines/boxing-insider-interview-with-floyd-mayweather-sr-in-between-one-to-twelve-hell-be-done-and-out/
- "A fighter without defense gets hit and gets beaten up, no matter how good he is" (radio interview, summary).
- "When the jab is working, everything comes off it. The jab is like the key." https://www.boxinginsider.com/headlines/boxing-insider-interview-with-floyd-mayweather-sr-in-between-one-to-twelve-hell-be-done-and-out/ (summary)
- Shoulder roll origin is disputed. Sr. says he picked a lot from Bob Tucker; Roger says Dale and Mitch in Kalamazoo. Jr.'s version: "timing and inches," stay in the pocket behind the lead shoulder. https://www.boxingscene.com/community/posts/664093
- Check hook: Jr. says it came from Michigan amateur trainers, not Sr. by name. Round 10 vs Hatton: check left hook as Hatton charged. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floyd_Mayweather_Jr._vs._Ricky_Hatton

### Reading an opponent
- No documented tells in his own words. Unverified.
- Pre-Hatton-Pacquiao: told Hatton to keep his hands up and not jump straight in at Pacquiao. http://boxingtalk.com/Roger-Mayweather-Is-There-A-Better-Mayweather-Than-Floyd-Sr-
- Observed that Roach was hesitant to let sparring partners hit Pacquiao to the body (2009 claim). https://m.lasvegassun.com/news/2009/apr/23/trainers-again-trade-verbal-jabs/

### Traps and baiting
- Mayweather style is the trap: stay in range, make the man miss, counter. Hatton chased, tired, and ate a check hook. https://3kingsboxing.com/look-back-mayweather-vs-hatton/
- Pull counter: pull away from a jab, load the back leg, fire the right. (Technique description, not a Sr. quote.) https://www.boxingscene.com/community/posts/664093

### Corner work
- Roach said Sr. "gets so nervous in between rounds he has trouble getting words out" (hostile source). https://www.pressreader.com/usa/los-angeles-times/20150501/282260959018089
- Boxing247 says Sr. had to work harder on in-fight strategy and motivation in De La Hoya's corner. https://www.boxing247.com/weblog/archives/123854
- Floyd Sr. in the crowd for Corrales: "keep the same approach, same pace." https://www.ringtv.com/616484-floyd-mayweather-diego-corrales-pretty-boy-perfection-20-years-later/

### Finishing / surviving
- Sr. said Zab Judah dropping Jr. with a body shot was "the biggest lie." No finishing rules documented. https://www.boxinginsider.com/headlines/boxing-insider-interview-with-floyd-mayweather-sr-in-between-one-to-twelve-hell-be-done-and-out/

### Fixing flaws / size
- Taught defense first, for days. No timeline beyond that. Size: unverified.
- "Go to the body, the head will follow" is NOT a Sr. quote. Wikipedia credits it to Nelson Mandela, advising Phillip N'dou against Jr. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floyd_Mayweather_Jr._vs._Phillip_N%27dou

## 3. Roger Mayweather

### Philosophy / system
- Took over as Jr.'s head trainer after the father-son split in 2000. Jr. said he had better chemistry with Roger. From then, more offense mixed with defense. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Mayweather
- Roach argued Roger was the better trainer, citing Roger's adjustments in the 2007 De La Hoya fight. https://ph.sports.yahoo.com/blogs/boxing/freddie-roach-blasts-floyd-mayweather-sr---says-floyd-jr--taught-himself-013843523.html
- Mitt work reads as long, rhythmic, cardio-style sessions that keep reactions sharp (critic's opinion). https://rossboxing.com/roger-mayweather-mitt-work/
- As a fighter: quick feet, sharp jab. Promo profile, weak. Roger's own technique teaching: unverified.

### Reading an opponent
- Hatton fight: Hatton chased and wore himself down; Roger kept Floyd on the same plan. https://3kingsboxing.com/look-back-mayweather-vs-hatton/
- Tells for hurt or tired: not documented. Unverified.

### Traps and baiting
- Leonard (Jr. vs Leonard report): between rounds told Floyd to move away "like Leonard did against Hagler." Source is a match report (spitting gusto). http://boxingtalk.com/Roger-Mayweather-Is-There-A-Better-Mayweather-Than-Floyd-Sr-
- Corrales: Floyd kept one pace and Corrales broke down by round 10, five knockdowns. https://www.ringtv.com/616484-floyd-mayweather-diego-corrales-pretty-boy-perfection-20-years-later/

### Corner work
- Hatton, between rounds: "Keep doing what you're doing, that's all you gotta do. That [expletive] can't out-fight you no way." Short, confident, same message each round. https://3kingsboxing.com/look-back-mayweather-vs-hatton/
- Style: few instructions, mostly reassurance when winning. Adjusting a losing fight: no documented example.

### Finishing / size
- Not documented. Unverified. Roger was suspended for the Baldomir fight, so not in the corner.

## 4. Nacho Beristain

### Philosophy / system
- Technician and counterpuncher school. Defense is "the most important thing in boxing"; named Marquez as an example. Does not force one style on every boxer (his words). Footwork first, then feet with hands. https://www.boxingscene.com/community/posts/558233 and https://boards.ie/discussion/2056555244/great-interview-with-nacho-beristain
- Promoter Arum calls him a control freak who imposes his style (critic). https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/arum-nacho-only-hurt-de-la-hoyas-training
- Hall of Fame 2011; began as a trainer with Mexico's Olympic team. https://tss.ib.tv/boxing/boxing-articles-2006/4480-beristain-humbled-by-hall-of-fame-honors

### Reading an opponent
- Diaz 2009: the team knew it needed heavy work to the midsection to wear Diaz down (2009 interview repost; search summary only). https://www.boxingscene.com/community/posts/558233
- Marquez said Nacho "can stop any hemorrhage" in the corner; in a later round Nacho went straight to a cut. https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/8924669/juan-manuel-marquez-reviews-career-defining-win-manny-pacquiao
- Tells list: not documented. Unverified.

### Traps and baiting
- Marquez vs Pacquiao IV: Pacquiao feints and steps in; Marquez takes his head off the line and fires a short right (stop-hit style). Marquez: "We always worked on that." The round-6 knockout came with one second left. https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/8924669/juan-manuel-marquez-reviews-career-defining-win-manny-pacquiao
- Marquez said in the first round they studied Pacquiao and used waist movement; after a combination in round 2 they adjusted again with waist movement and switched to right hands and hooks. Marquez, not Nacho, said it. https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/8924669/juan-manuel-marquez-reviews-career-defining-win-manny-pacquiao
- "Studied Pacquiao's tape for patterns": weak source, unverified.

### Corner work
- Marquez waited for the corner to "let Beristain do his job." Beristain's role: calm repair and adjustment. Instruction count: unverified.
- Diaz fight: Diaz asked Nacho after a round whether he was ahead while bleeding. Marquez finished with a ninth-round right uppercut. https://tss.ib.tv/boxing/boxing-articles-2009/6602-juan-manuel-magnificent-marquez-kos-diaz

### Finishing / surviving, size
- Marquez-Diaz shows patience: survive early pressure, win late. No stated rule. Size: unverified.
- Note: Nacho did not train Chavez Sr. (only Chavez Jr. for the 2017 Canelo fight). https://www.spin.ph/boxing/legendary-mexican-trainer-nacho-beristain-julio-cesar-chavez

## 5. Abel Sanchez

### Philosophy / system
- Trains to his man's strengths, not the opponent's weaknesses (Stevens camp). https://tss.ib.tv/boxing/boxing-articles/17709-abel-sanchez-on-the-boxer-trainer-relationship
- Close trainer-fighter bond so the fighter "doesn't have to think for himself." Same link.
- Big Bear altitude camp, "The Summit" gym; heavy bag, uppercut bag, mitts for power. Fitness-blog level source. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/how-to-train-like-boxing-world-champion-gennadiy-ggg-golovkin/
- Wants a versatile fighter. Murray fight (11 rounds) was useful: "gave us canvas to practice" gym skills under real combat. https://en.tengrinews.kz/sport/murray-gave-us-canvas-to-practice-abel-sanchez-259350/site.Presidential elections link

### Reading an opponent
- Canelo: Sanchez said Canelo could go to the body but has to stand still to do it, so going to the body exposes him. https://www.boxingscene.com/sanchez-ggg-go-body-canelo-nearly-running--124429
- Geale: told Golovkin in rounds 1-3 to stop hunting the head and work the shoulders/upper body, the opening would come. Golovkin didn't follow it. https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/sanchez-i-give-golovkin-9-out-10-geale-win
- Tells for hurt or tired: not documented. Unverified.

### Traps and baiting
- "The Ginger Snap": the Hopkins liver shot on De La Hoya, "freshened up" for Canelo II. https://www.badlefthook.com/2018/9/11/17845752/abel-sanchez-hints-at-golovkins-game-plan-for-canelo-rematch
- Reality check: Canelo out-landed GGG 46 to 6 to the body in the rematch. The plan on paper did not happen. Same link.
- General liver-shot setup (not Sanchez): throw a hook to the head so he expects it, then go to the liver. https://evolve-mma.com/blog/5-setups-for-the-liver-hook-in-boxing/

### Corner work
- Told Golovkin in Canelo I he was losing, as motivation, whispered in his ear (even though he knew he was miked). https://remezcla.com/features/sports/canelo-alvarez-golovkin-draw/
- Instruction count: unverified.

### Finishing / surviving
- On Stevens: "when he steps through the ropes he wants to hurt you." Golovkin stood in front of Stevens, picked him apart, corner stopped it after 8. https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/23384136/abel-sanchez-once-fight-gennady-golovkin-never-going-same
- Murray: Sanchez felt the corner could have stopped it earlier because Murray took heavy punishment. https://en.tengrinews.kz/sport/murray-gave-us-canvas-to-practice-abel-sanchez-259350/site.Presidential elections link

### Fixing flaws / size
- Fixing flaws and size: no documented method or timeline. Unverified.

## 6. Buddy McGirt

- The jab is the base. From Ray Arcel: "I had about four world champions and all they had was the left jab. I educated their left hand." McGirt had Kovalev throw only jabs for some rounds, jab-only bag rounds: "everything else is going to follow." Keep it basic, perfect it. https://www.ringtv.com/?p=555950
- Al Certo, his manager: the jab busts you up, but "the hook is the coup de grace." https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/VA-news/VA-Pilot/issues/1994/vp940925/09250157.htm
- Skipped watching tape of Kovalev's loss; trained basics. https://www.ringtv.com/?p=555950

### Reading an opponent
- Not documented. Unverified.

### Traps and baiting
- Not documented. Gatti rematch: "Arturo won the rematch by throwing combinations and getting out." https://boxingnewsonline.net/buddy-mcgirt-looks-back-on-his-career-as-both-a-boxer-and-a-coach
- Gatti's boxer plan in Ward I was dropped early in the fight; Gatti reverted to brawling. Sources disagree on whether McGirt was in the corner for fight 1. https://www.boxingscene.com/ward-surprised-arturo-gattis-trainer--8328

### Finishing / surviving
- A hurt fighter on the road back: see the Gatti Ward I item. Rule of the story: a man can be saved by a long rest, not by tactics. Anything more: unverified.

### Fixing flaws / size
- Takes fighters right after a loss (Gatti, Forrest, Tarver, Kovalev) and rebuilds from the jab. Time: unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_McGirt
- Size: unverified.

## 7. Robert Garcia

### Philosophy / system
- Fixed Maidana's footwork and wide-swinging punches: tighter defense, crisper punches, "suffocating pressure that cut off the ring." Maidana threw 101 body shots vs Broner. https://www.ringtv.com/?p=182623 and https://www.ringtv.com/?p=182595

- Fighter must be coachable; Garcia credits Maidana's willingness to listen. https://www.ringtv.com/?p=182623

The pattern was borrowed from De La Hoya's 2007 attempt. https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/el-entrenador-de-marcos-maidana-ya-entramos-en-guerra-nid1726700/ and https://mstg.sherdog.com/news/articles/Boxing-Marcos-Maidana-and-the-Oscar-De-La-Hoya-Blueprint-73647

- Tells: not documented. Unverified.

### Traps and baiting
- Not documented. Unverified.

## 8. Ronnie Shields

### Philosophy / system
- "It all starts with the jab." The opponent has to figure out "how do I get away from that jab?" Camp drills let speed carry the power, not knockout reaching. https://cdn.premierboxingchampions.com/node/32032
- Fighter-first. He defended Charlo's squared stance ("works for Jermall, may not work for somebody else"). https://www.premierboxingchampions.com/node/27741
- Whitaker's jab: about 85 percent accurate, used to find the distance for combinations. https://www.si.com/fannation/boxing/ronnie-shields-reveals-floyd-mayweather-move-terence-crawford-could-use-beat-canelo-alvarez

### Reading an opponent
- Spence-Pacquiao: both southpaws, so stance changes the jab's value; experience over age; both strong chins so a knockout was unlikely. https://www.badlefthook.com/2021/7/20/22586194/ronnie-shields-breaks-down-manny-pacquiao-vs-errol-spence-boxing-news-2021
- Tells for hurt: not documented. The URL "he left, see it in his eyes" is about Charlo's mental recovery. Unverified for fight tells.

### Traps and baiting
- Not documented. Mayweather vs Canelo: Mayweather stepped around pressure, forced Canelo to reset. https://www.si.com/fannation/boxing/ronnie-shields-reveals-floyd-mayweather-move-terence-crawford-could-use-beat-canelo-alvarez

### Corner work
- Not documented. Shields worked with Tyson, Holyfield, Whitaker, Forrest, Lara, Charlo, Spence, Joshua briefly. https://boxrec.com/wiki/index.php/Ronnie_Shields
- Also helped Charlo's mind: brought in Roy Jones Jr. as a respected voice. https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/trainer-shields-on-jermall-charlo-hes-back-he-he-left-see-it-his-eyes
- Spence: loss would end his career, said before camp. https://www.yardbarker.com/boxing/articles/spence_will_walk_away_if_he_loses_to_tszyu/s1_17349_44088062

### Finishing / surviving
- Charlo-Canelo: Shields said "just don't think about the judges, how they're scoring." Plan was to get to a range where he could take the gas out of Canelo. https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/shields-charlo-just-not-think-about-judges-how-theyre-scoring-canelo-fight

### Size
- Crawford vs Canelo: "Knowledge is King." A smaller man doesn't need KO power; move, step around pressure. Weight gap not a big issue (Canelo about 190 walking around, Crawford about 10 lb less). But earn the bigger man's respect, don't bike for 12. Same SI link.

## For Last Bell

Tags: R Roach, FM Sr., RM Roger, NB Beristain, AS Sanchez, BM McGirt, RG Garcia, RS Shields. **[design]** = my inference, not a documented trainer quote. Numbers are starting values for the sim, not measurements.

### Brain: habits and traps
1. **Habit bait (R).** If the opponent has shown the same pre-punch tell 3 or more times in a fight (a hand pump, a chin lift, a cocked hook), the brain fires its counter on the tell. Hatton: cocked left hook gave the right hook. Rough: after 3 sightings, counter chance on the tell 40-60%, else normal. Memory should live in the fight (a tally per opponent tell), reset on a new fight unless L4c rematch memory is on.
2. **Counter the counter (NB).** When a feint-and-step-in man is spotted (2+ feint-in sequences), take the head off the line and fire a short straight, a stop-hit style. Marquez-Pacquiao IV. Counters should land only if the fighter has timing skill; give the counter a bonus of about +15% to land vs a feint-in man at Pro and higher.
3. **Pull counter, high-defense man (FM).** After a jab, a defensive fighter pulls back and fires the right: do it if his defense skill is high and the opponent's jab is slow. Use it only when the opponent has missed 2 jabs in a row.
4. **Check hook vs a charger (FM).** If the opponent has rushed in 3+ times in 20 seconds, throw a check hook as he arrives (Mayweather-Hatton round 10). Charger loses about 1 point of balance on a hit. Counter chance 25-35% per rush for defensive styles.
5. **Wait on the drill (R).** A man with a known habit gets drilled on it in the AI corner's plan ("watch his left hook, then right hook over it"). Corner note for rounds 1-3 only; if not working after round 4, drop it. Roach's 3-4-round trial is [design].

### Brain: body, hurt, tired
6. **Body invites the head (NB, RG, AS, RS) [design for the numbers].** Body shots on one man 3 or more times in a round: his guard drops by a few percent per body hit, so head chance rises. Rough: guard height down about 5% per landed body shot, to a limit of 20%. This follows the Diaz plan (midsection to wear him down) and Maidana's 101 body blows vs Broner.
7. **Hurt-man bluff check [design].** A hurt man who says he is fine (stands tall, throws back) still shows: slow hands, shorter steps, more clinches. A veteran-level brain (Pro and up) treats "two of three" signs as hurt even if the opponent keeps working: legs short and hands late. If the opponent's recent stamina or damage says hurt, treat him as hurt regardless of how he acts. Rough: a vet acts on 2 signs; a novice needs 3 or a visible stagger.
8. **Tired man's hands drop [design].** Late in a round or fight, a gassed man's guard height falls and counter speed slows. Rough: the brain throws a right hand to a head that is open after 2 punches into the gloves; trigger when the opponent's stamina is under about 30%.
9. **Don't gas out finishing (R).** Pacquiao hurt Horn in round 9 and then spent himself. A hunter with stamina under about 40% should keep two or three-punch bursts and a rest step, not an all-out flurry, unless the hurt man is nearly out (stagger or knockdown just happened). Retire the all-out flurry when gas is under 25% and the target is not already down or reeling.
10. **Finish the staggered man, but step in under his punch (R, RG).** After a knockdown or a stagger: back him up (RG: "let's start backing him up") and keep the jab-hook going. Survival: if hurt, go under the overhand, don't pull away straight back (R at Marquez IV: that is how Pacquiao got dropped).

### Brain: styles
11. **Jab is the base (BM, RS, FM).** A fighter with a good jab should throw it first and use it to set range; lower skill fighters get the "jab-only rounds" drill in the learning step (L4). Use the jab to find the range before combinations. About 40% of punches thrown should be jabs for a boxer style. Charlo and Whitaker lines are about accuracy, not volume.
12. **Pressure to the ropes, body first, lighter and quicker (RG).** Bigger puncher vs smaller, quicker man: cut the ring, pin him on the ropes, work the body. Drop weight/gas cost rather than power: keep pace up 12 rounds.
13. **Smaller man: step around, don't run (RS, R).** A smaller fighter vs a bigger man should step around pressure and make him reset, but not bike for 12 rounds; earn respect with a few hard shots (RS). Roach: don't stand in front of the bigger man, don't get cornered.
14. **Easy flaws in weaker fighters (R, FM, RG) [design for numbers].** The simplest learnable flaws: telegraphing a hook (cocking it), wide swinging punches, no jab, chin up, head straight back on a jab. A brain at Pro or above marks a weak fighter's repeated habit after about 3 sightings. A flaw fixed by a coach: footwork first (NB, RG), jab second (BM, RS), defense first for the young (FM).

### Corner AI
15. **Short and few (R, RM).** Corner between-round talk: one main instruction, one reinforcement, two at most. Roach: only the seconds before the bell. One voice in the corner (R). If winning: one reassurance line (RM: "keep doing what you doing").
16. **Stay with the plan when it works (RM, RG, FM).** If the fighter is ahead on the cards, the corner says stay with it (no new instruction). Only change the plan if two rounds are lost.
17. **Plan abandoned = loss (R, De La Hoya 2007).** Roach's jab plan worked until De La Hoya dropped it. If the fighter has dropped the jab (count of jabs below half his usual for a round) while ahead, the corner's first line is "go back to the jab."
18. **Telling a man he's losing (AS).** A corner may say "you're losing" as a motivator even when he isn't (AS, Canelo I). Use rarely; effect should be small (about +5% work rate for a round) and risks a worse read. Treat it as a bluff the fighter can't verify.
19. **Corner stop call (RG, R, AS).** The fighter will not let the corner stop it (Margarito); the AI corner should have a threshold for a stoppage: eye closed plus 3+ rounds lost plus hurt in the round. Garcia's critics: a late stop can cost the fighter's career. A late-stop corner gets a "late" flag (like REFS stop -1) with more damage taken.
20. **Fighter-first plan (AS, RS, NB).** The AI corner should adjust the plan to the fighter's own strengths (AS, RS) more than to the opponent's weaknesses. Rough: weight the plan 60/40 toward own strengths.
