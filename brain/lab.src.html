How to read it. The game only tells each brain what's true in the ring. The brain decides when it notices a punch, what it thinks it is, and what to do. "(it was a hook)" means he misread it. "bit on a feint" means he bought it. Punches are trainers' numbers: 1 jab, 2 right hand, 3 lead hook, 4 rear hook, 5 lead uppercut, 6 rear uppercut, b = to the body.
